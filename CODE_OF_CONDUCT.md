# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors.

## Our Standards

- Be respectful and inclusive in communication
- Accept constructive criticism professionally
- Focus on what is best for the community
- Show empathy towards other community members

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Publishing others' private information
- Other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Reporting

If you experience or witness violations of this code of conduct, please open an issue or contact the maintainers.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this code of conduct and will handle reported violations appropriately.
